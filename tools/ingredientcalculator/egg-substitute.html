How to Choose the Right Egg Substitute

The best egg substitute depends on what role eggs play in your recipe. In cookies and brownies, eggs act as binders — flax eggs or chia eggs work great. In cakes, eggs provide leavening — try aquafaba or commercial egg replacers. In moist quick breads, eggs add moisture — use applesauce or mashed banana.

Can I use multiple egg substitutes in one recipe?

Yes, but be cautious. When replacing more than 3 eggs, you may want to combine substitutes (e.g., use flax for binding + extra baking powder for lift). Results vary — recipes with 4+ eggs (like angel food cake) are harder to make egg-free.

Will egg substitutes change the taste?

Some will. Banana and applesauce add subtle flavor. Flax eggs add a slight nutty taste. Aquafaba and commercial replacers are the most neutral options for flavor-sensitive recipes.
