What are baking ratios?

Baking ratios express the proportion of key ingredients by weight. The classic pound cake ratio is 1:1:1:1, equal parts butter, sugar, eggs, and flour. Once you know the ratio, you can scale to any size by adjusting the total weight.

Why bake by weight?

Weight-based baking is far more precise than volume measurements. A cup of flour can vary by 30+ grams depending on how it is scooped. Using a kitchen scale and ratios gives consistent, professional results every time.

Can I modify these ratios?

Yes. These are starting-point formulas. You can adjust ratios slightly based on preference, a bit more butter for richness, less sugar for a less sweet result. Just keep the proportions balanced so the chemistry still works.
