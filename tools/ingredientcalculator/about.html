What I build

One job per tool, done fast

Most cooking sites bury a simple converter under ads and a thousand-word essay about someone's grandmother. I go the other way. Each tool does one thing and loads right away.

The cups to grams converter carries a real density for every ingredient, because a cup of flour and a cup of honey weigh nothing alike. The nutrition estimator runs on USDA numbers. The scaler handles the awkward parts of doubling a recipe, like what to do with three eggs when you need four and a half.

I made the whole site on my own and I work on it full time. That's the only reason the tools stay quick and a feature request from a stranger actually gets read the same day.

The honest part

What works, and what didn't

Not every tool lands. Some I built, watched nobody open, and quietly pulled down. The ones that stay are the ones people come back to.

So I'd rather hear what's broken than guess at it. If a number looks off, or you want an ingredient added, or a tool would save you a step, send it over. Small fixes usually ship the same week, and the bigger ideas go on a list I actually work through.
