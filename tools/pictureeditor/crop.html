What aspect ratios are best for social media?

Instagram posts: 1:1 (square) or 4:5 (portrait). Stories/Reels: 9:16. Twitter/Facebook link previews: 1.91:1. YouTube thumbnails: 16:9. Pinterest: 2:3. LinkedIn: 1.91:1 or 1:1.

Does cropping reduce image quality?

Cropping removes pixels from the edges but does not recompress the remaining image data. The cropped area retains its original quality. For best results, start with the highest resolution image available.

Can I crop to a custom size?

Yes. Select "Free" mode and drag any corner or edge to create a custom crop region. You can also drag the selection area to reposition it.
