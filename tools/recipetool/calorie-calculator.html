About this calculator: Uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, considered the most accurate BMR formula for most people. BMR = (10 x weight in kg) + (6.25 x height in cm) - (5 x age) +/- gender factor. TDEE is BMR multiplied by your activity level. Results are estimates; individual metabolism varies. Consult a healthcare provider for personalized advice.
