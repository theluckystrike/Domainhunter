How to use these guides together

These three tools are designed to work as a system for anyone who cooks at home. Start with the weekly meal planning template or the more detailed meal prep calculator to decide what you will eat and generate a shopping list. Then run a few recipes through the food cost calculator to see exactly what each serving costs and where your grocery money goes.

Together they help you eat more consistently, waste less food, and spend less. Because everything runs locally in your browser, there is no signup and nothing you enter ever leaves your device. For nutrition on a specific recipe, pair these with the recipe nutrition calculator to get calories and macros per serving.

Frequently asked questions

Are these cooking guides and calculators free?

Yes — every guide and calculator is completely free with no signup and no account. The tools run entirely in your browser, so nothing you type is uploaded or stored. Use the food cost, meal prep, and meal planning tools as often as you like.

Which guide should I start with?

To save money, start with the food cost calculator to see cost per serving. To eat more consistently, use the meal prep calculator to plan a week and generate a shopping list. The printable meal planning template is best when you want a simple weekly menu on paper.

Can I print or save the meal plans?

Yes. The meal prep calculator and weekly meal planning template both include print-friendly layouts, so you can print a clean copy or save it as a PDF. Shopping lists can also be copied to your clipboard for the store.
