## WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

1. **Backorders are passive.** Once placed, the platforms automatically attempt to catch each domain at the moment it drops. No further action needed until catch or auction.

2. **If caught uncontested:** You get an email from the platform. The domain is registered in your name. You pay the backorder price ($59/$79/~$11 depending on platform).

3. **If caught and contested (multiple backorders exist):**
   - **DropCatch:** Public 3-day auction. Anyone can bid. Starts at $59.
   - **SnapNames:** Private 3-day auction. Only backorder holders can bid. Starts at $79.
   - **Dynadot:** Private 7-10 day auction. Only backorder holders can bid.

4. **If NOT caught:** $0 charged. The domain was caught by another service or renewed by the owner.

5. **Monitor GoDaddy Auctions** weekly for any of the 9 domains appearing in their expired auction pipeline (relevant only if the domain is registered at GoDaddy).
