## GoDaddy Expiration Timeline (Most Registrars Similar)

```
Day 0        — Domain expires
Day 0-18     — Grace period (owner can renew at normal price)
Day 19-25    — Registrar hold (owner can renew with fee, ~$80)
Day 26-35    — Registrar auction (GoDaddy Auctions, Expired Domains)
Day 36-41    — Closeout/redemption (final chance, ~$100+ fee)
Day 42+      — Pending delete → public drop (5-day ICANN window)
```

**clientRenewProhibited** (taskplanner.com): This flag means the registrar has locked renewal. The domain WILL drop unless the owner contacts registrar support. High probability of release.

## Platform 1: DropCatch.com (PRIMARY)

### Account Setup
1. Go to **DropCatch.com**
2. Click **"Register"** — create account with primary email
3. Verify email address
4. Add payment method (credit card required for auction deposits)
5. Complete identity verification if prompted

### Placing Backorders
1. Log in to DropCatch.com
2. Click **"Backorder"** in the top navigation
3. In the search field, enter the domain name (e.g., `aidevtools.com`)
4. Click **"Add Backorder"** or **"Place Backorder"**
5. Confirm the backorder — no charge is applied at this stage
6. Repeat for each domain:
   - `aidevtools.com`
   - `bestdevtools.com`
   - `finetuneai.com`
   - `taskplanner.com`
7. Verify all backorders appear in your **"My Backorders"** dashboard

### Cost Structure
- **Placing backorder**: FREE
- **If caught (no competition)**: $59
- **If caught (multiple bidders)**: Goes to DropCatch auction, starts at $59, no max
- **Auction duration**: 3 days from catch

### What Happens at Drop
- DropCatch runs high-speed catching servers
- At the moment the domain enters public availability, DropCatch attempts to register
- If successful and you're the only backorder: you win at $59
- If multiple backorders exist: 3-day auction among all who backordered

## Platform 2: SnapNames.com (BACKUP/INSURANCE)

### Account Setup
1. Go to **SnapNames.com**
2. Click **"Sign Up"** / **"Create Account"**
3. Enter email, create password
4. Verify email
5. Add credit card for auction participation
6. Agree to terms of service

### Placing Backorders
1. Log in to SnapNames.com
2. Use the search bar — enter domain name
3. Click **"Backorder"** next to the domain
4. Set your maximum bid (recommended: $69-100 for each)
5. Confirm backorder placement
6. Repeat for each domain:
   - `aidevtools.com`
   - `bestdevtools.com`
   - `finetuneai.com`
   - `taskplanner.com`
7. Check **"My Backorders"** to confirm all are active

### Cost Structure
- **Placing backorder**: FREE
- **If caught (no competition)**: $69
- **If caught (multiple bidders)**: SnapNames auction, starting at $69
- **Auction duration**: Typically 3 days
