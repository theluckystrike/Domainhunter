## Platform 1: SnapNames

**URL:** https://www.snapnames.com
**Backorder Price:** $79 (only charged if caught)
**Payment:** Credit card required on file
**Deposit:** None

### Signup Steps

1. Go to https://www.snapnames.com
2. Click **Sign Up / Register**
3. Create username and password
4. Choose a **bid alias** (this is PUBLIC -- do not use your real name)
5. Enter contact information (name, address, phone)
6. Enter credit card information
7. Verify your email address
8. Account is active immediately

### How to Place a Backorder

1. Use the search bar to find your target domain
2. Click **Backorder** to add it to your cart
3. Complete checkout -- you will NOT be charged now
4. View your orders in **Buyer Dashboard > Pending Orders**
5. SnapNames monitors the domain and notifies you when it becomes available

### What Happens When Caught

- **You're the only backorder:** Domain is yours at $79
- **Multiple backorders exist:** 3-day private auction among backorder holders only

### Key Tips

- **CRITICAL:** SnapNames and NameJet share identical inventory since 2020. NEVER backorder the same domain on both platforms -- you will bid against yourself.
- Lower catch rate than DropCatch (fewer registrar accreditations)
- Unlimited free backorders (no charge until you win)
- Keep credit card valid -- failed payment = lost domain

## Platform 2: Dynadot

**URL:** https://www.dynadot.com
**Backorder Price:** $24.99 standard (cheapest platform)
**Payment:** Credit card, PayPal, wire transfer, Alipay
**Minimum Deposit:** $5.00 (REQUIRED before placing backorders)

### Signup Steps

1. Go to https://www.dynadot.com
2. Click **Create Account**
3. Enter email and password
4. Complete contact information
5. Verify email address
6. **IMPORTANT:** Go to **Account > Add Funds**
7. Add **$5 minimum** via credit card or PayPal
8. Account is now backorder-ready

### Backorder Price Tiers

| Tier | Price | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Standard | $24.99 | Default -- attempts to catch at release |
| Low Priority | $64.99 | Slightly better catch attempt |
| High Priority | $124.99 | Attempts to catch before release |

**Recommendation:** Use Standard ($24.99) for all domains. High priority is not worth the premium given Dynadot's lower catch rate.

### How to Place a Backorder

1. Navigate to **Market > Backorder** (https://www.dynadot.com/market/backorder)
2. Search for the target domain
3. Click **Place Backorder**
4. Select priority level (use Standard)
5. Confirm -- no charge until domain is caught

### What Happens When Caught

- **You're the only backorder:** Domain is yours at backorder price
- **Multiple backorders exist:** 7-day PUBLIC auction (anyone can join, not just backorder holders)

### Key Tips

- Cheapest platform -- good as supplementary coverage
- Only ~15 registrar accreditations vs DropCatch's 1,200+ (lower catch rate)
- Public auctions mean anyone can pile on -- less control than SnapNames
- Must have $5 balance BEFORE placing any backorders
- Mixed user reviews on catch success -- do not rely on Dynadot alone

## Platform 3: NameJet

**URL:** https://www.namejet.com
**Backorder Price:** $79 (minimum bid $69)
**Payment:** Credit card, wire transfer
**Deposit:** None

### RECOMMENDATION: SKIP NameJet

NameJet shares identical inventory with SnapNames since 2020. If you sign up for SnapNames, NameJet is redundant. Signing up for both risks accidentally bidding against yourself.

### If You Do Sign Up

1. Go to https://www.namejet.com
2. Click **Register**
3. Create username, password, bid alias (same system as SnapNames)
4. Enter contact and credit card info
5. Verify email

### Critical: Pre-Bid System

NameJet's backorder system works differently from what you might expect:

- **Your backorder amount IS your starting bid** -- it is NOT a proxy bid
- If you set $79 and you're the only backorder, you pay $79
- If you set $200 and you're the only backorder, you pay $200
- **Always set to minimum ($69) unless you have a specific reason to bid higher**
- If multiple backorders exist, a 3-day private auction begins where proxy bidding IS used

## Platform 4: GoDaddy Auctions

**URL:** https://auctions.godaddy.com
**Membership:** $4.99/year
**Payment:** Credit card, PayPal

### How to Join (Within Existing GoDaddy Account)

1. Log in to your GoDaddy account at godaddy.com
2. Navigate to https://auctions.godaddy.com
3. Click **Join Now**
4. Review and accept the Auctions Membership Agreement
5. Pay $4.99 membership fee
6. Active immediately

### How Expired Domain Auctions Work

| Day After Expiry | What Happens |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Day 0 | Domain expires |
| Day 1-25 | Grace period (registrant can renew) |
| Day 26-36 | Listed on GoDaddy Auctions (10-day auction) |
| Day 30-36 | Registrant CANNOT renew if there's an active bid |
| Day 37-42 | Final Closeout auction (5 days, lower competition) |
| Day 43 | Auction ends -- winner gets domain or it releases to registry |

### Key Tips

- GoDaddy eliminated backorders in 2024-2025 -- auctions are the ONLY option now
- Only GoDaddy-registered domains appear in expired auctions -- limited inventory
- Lower priority than DropCatch/SnapNames for targeted domains
- Closeout auctions (Day 37-42) often have much lower competition
- Payment due within 48 hours of winning
- Worth the $4.99 for browsing access and opportunistic buys
