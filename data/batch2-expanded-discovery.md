## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Searched across 4 keyword clusters (AI/LLM, Developer Tools, SaaS/Product, Comparison/Review) using web searches, ExpiredDomains.net, Wayback CDX, WHOIS lookups, and startup graveyard research. Key findings:

- **Most high-value AI/dev keyword .com domains are registered** and held by speculators or active projects

- **Several known shut-down AI startups** still hold their domains (codeparrot.ai, tune.ai, subtl.ai) -- these are WATCH LIST targets for when domains drop
- **Fresh unregistered domains** exist but have zero authority (no backlinks) -- only useful for new brand plays
- **Domains approaching expiry** in 2026 represent the best near-term opportunities

## SECTION 2: MARKET INTELLIGENCE

### Startup Failure Trends (2024-2025)
- **966 startups** shut down in 2024 (25.6% increase over 2023)
- **B2B SaaS** shutdowns rose from 5.2% to 7.7%
- **Developer tools** shutdowns rose from 6.0% to 6.4%
- **AI wrapper companies** saw the sharpest increase — most had "API access" but no proprietary data advantage
- Series A failures jumped from ~6% to ~14% of all shutdowns (2.5x increase)

### Domain Market Trends
- .io faces governance uncertainty from Chagos sovereignty transfer — some companies moving away
- .ai registrations at 807K+ and climbing ($140-200 for 2yr minimum)
- .app passed .io as largest developer TLD
- .dev is smallest but fastest-growing developer TLD (+45%)
- Google's 2024-2025 updates penalize "repurposed" authority sites that change dramatically in niche
- AI agents increasingly value "semantically clear" domains that can be categorized as high-confidence sources

### Key Recommendation
The **highest-value strategy** is monitoring shut-down startup domains (Category A) for when they actually drop. A domain like **builder.ai** (expiring June 2026, $1.5B company, Forbes/TechCrunch backlinks) would be extremely valuable if it drops — but likely to be caught by professional drop-catching services. Set backordering alerts on all Category A and B domains.

## SECTION 3: SEARCH METHODOLOGY NOTES

### What Worked
- WHOIS lookups to verify registration status and expiry dates
- Cross-referencing startup graveyard lists (Failory, TechStartups, Inc42) with domain availability
- Checking CDX/Wayback archive depth for content potential
- Checking domains with near-term expiry dates for the keyword clusters

### Limitations
- ExpiredDomains.net member search requires login (could not scrape keyword searches)
- DomCop, SpamZilla require paid accounts for filtered searches
- Wayback Machine CDX API was intermittently returning 503/504 errors
- Cannot predict which domains will actually be renewed vs. dropped
- Backlink/DR metrics require Ahrefs/Moz/Majestic paid tools for verification
