## CRITICAL WARNING: SnapNames + NameJet Share Inventory

Since 2020, NameJet and SnapNames operate on a SHARED inventory pool. If you place a backorder on the SAME domain from both platforms, you will bid against yourself in the auction. **Pick ONE platform per domain — never both.**

Strategy: Use SnapNames for some domains, NameJet for others, to diversify catch attempts across their shared pool while also using DropCatch and Dynadot as independent catchers.

## PLATFORM 3: NameJet.com

### IMPORTANT: Shares inventory with SnapNames
DO NOT backorder the same domain on BOTH NameJet and SnapNames. Split them.

### Account Setup (5 minutes)
1. Go to https://www.namejet.com
2. Click "Register" / "Create Account"
3. Enter: username, password, bid alias, contact info, billing info
4. Verify email
5. Ready to place backorders immediately

### How to Place a Backorder
1. Log in at https://www.namejet.com
2. Search for the domain name
3. Click **"Backorder"** to add to cart, or click domain name for details
4. Set your **maximum proxy bid** (this is the max you'll pay in auction)
5. Check out
6. If you're the only backorderer: you win at minimum bid (~$69)
7. If multiple backorderers: 3-day private auction begins, proxy bidding up to your max

### Pricing
- **$0 to place** a backorder
- **$69 minimum catch price** (sole backorderer)
- **Auction price varies** (if competition, goes to 3-day private auction)
- Proxy bidding: set max, NameJet auto-bids minimum increments for you
- All prices include 1-year registration

### How to Search and Bid

- **Expired Domains:** Listed 26-29 days after expiry for 10-day auction

## TRADEMARK WARNINGS

### reggiewatts.com
**HIGH RISK:** Reggie Watts is a living public figure (comedian, musician, bandleader on The Late Late Show). Using this domain could trigger:
- UDRP complaint (Uniform Domain-Name Dispute-Resolution Policy)
- Trademark infringement claims
- Forced transfer without refund
**Recommendation:** Only backorder if you have a legitimate non-infringing use. Do NOT use for a redirect site or ad parking.

### ghostautonomy.com
**MODERATE RISK:** Ghost Autonomy was a VC-backed startup that shut down in April 2024. The "Ghost" trademark may have been sold during asset liquidation. The company raised ~$220M and was backed by OpenAI. Check if any trademark assignment occurred during wind-down proceedings before aggressive bidding.

## MONITORING PLAN

### Daily Checks
1. Log into each platform daily to check backorder status
2. Watch for auction notifications (email alerts)
3. Monitor DropCatch verification progress

### If a Domain is Caught
1. You'll receive email notification from the catching platform
2. If sole backorderer: pay catch price, domain is yours
3. If auction starts: set your MAX BID per sprint8_max_bids.json
4. Do NOT exceed max bids — discipline prevents overspending
5. Pay within 48 hours of winning

### If a Domain is NOT Caught
1. Check if another service caught it (might appear on their auction)
2. Search Afternic, Sedo, and other aftermarket platforms
3. Consider direct outreach to new registrant if valuable enough
