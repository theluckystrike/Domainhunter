# Funny / Meme / Internet Culture Word List for Domain Hunting

**Date:** 2026-05-01
**Purpose:** Curated funny, absurd, and internet culture words (3-6 letters) optimized to avoid premium domain pricing
**Total words:** 100

---

## Why This Works

Premium domain surcharges target **aspirational, brandable, and tech-forward words** -- the kinds of words startups and agencies fight over. Words like "epic," "zen," "bliss," "flux," and "nexus" get $200-$2,000+ premiums because they appear on every registry's "desirable word" list.

**Funny/absurd words dodge premium lists because:**
1. No brand manager puts "burp" or "spleen" on their shortlist
2. Onomatopoeia words are inherently memorable (the k/plosive sound effect)
3. They create viral-worthy domain combos that people share for laughs
4. Internet culture words are too niche/recent for registry premium databases
5. Old-timey insults are real dictionary words that nobody thinks to monetize

### Premium Avoidance Confidence

These words are specifically chosen to be **real English words that no brand manager would ever shortlist**. The premium detection algorithms used by registries (Donuts, Identity Digital, etc.) focus on:

- Aspirational adjectives (pure, bold, epic) -- **none in this list**
- Tech/startup buzzwords (data, cloud, hub) -- **none in this list**
- Common brand components (star, wolf, flame) -- **none in this list**
- Short common nouns with commercial value (gold, wine, cash) -- **none in this list**

Instead, every word here falls into categories that registries ignore:
- Onomatopoeia (not brandable)
- Insults (negative connotation = no premium)
- Body parts (gross = no premium)
- Internet slang (too niche for registry databases)
- Yiddish/British slang (regional, not mainstream commercial)

**Estimated premium hit rate: <5%** (possible exceptions: "dork" and "goon" which have some pop culture presence, and "nerf" which is a brand name)
