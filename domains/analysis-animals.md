## Why This Works

Premium domain lists are built from **brandable buzzwords**: adjectives (bold, pure, soft, lush), materials (gold, silk), nature-aspirational words (wave, glow, rose, star). These get $200-$2,000+ surcharges across .hair, .skin, .beauty, .quest, .mom, .best, .beer, .monster, .surf, .garden.

**Uncommon animal names dodge premium lists because:**
1. They are real dictionary words (pass the "is this a word?" test for brandability)
2. They are NOT on marketing/brand word lists that registries use
3. They have natural appeal -- animals evoke emotion, memorability, identity
4. Many create compelling brand combos: `vole.garden`, `moth.quest`, `stoat.monster`, `heron.surf`

**Confirmed non-premium precedents:** fawn (9 cheap TLDs), wren (6 cheap), curl (7 cheap), jade (3 cheap)
**Confirmed premium traps:** wolf, fox, hawk, bear, lion -- too iconic/brandable
