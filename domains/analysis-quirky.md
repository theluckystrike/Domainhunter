## Strategy

Premium domain pricing targets words with **immediate commercial brand appeal** -- words any startup founder would want (epic, boss, zen, swift, rush). This list targets the opposite end: words that are **memorable, personality-rich, and slightly odd** -- the kind of words that make someone pause, smile, or feel curious. They fly under premium radar because registrars price based on demand from corporate buyers, not quirky indie projects.

The sweet spot: words that are **known enough to be understood** but **unusual enough that no domain registrar flags them as premium**. A word like "wonky" is universally understood but no Fortune 500 company wants wonky.com, so wonky.lol stays at base price.

## Premium Risk Assessment

**Low risk (safe bets):** braw, dreich, bairn, daggy, arvo, naff, manky, narky, bumf, bothy, frowzy, rufous, nubby, finial, newel, soffit, lintel, sconce, dormer, gasket, shim, rebar, bezel, caulk
- These are obscure enough that registrar algorithms won't flag them

**Medium risk (watch for):** nifty, swell, zany, plucky, dapper, snazzy, tweed, suede, mauve, rivet
- Known words with some lifestyle/brand appeal; most should still be base price on niche TLDs (.lol, .quest, .monster) but could be premium on .com/.io

**Words deliberately excluded for premium risk:** slick, denim, silky, velvet, satin, lace, feisty, brash, brazen, cheeky, muslin, burlap
