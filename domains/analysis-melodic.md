## Strategy

Premium pricing correlates with **brand recognition and common usage**, not phonetic beauty. The sweet spot is words that *sound* gorgeous but are rarely used as brand names or product names. This list avoids confirmed-premium patterns (simple Anglo-Saxon monosyllables like "bold", "rush", "star") and leans into:

- **Latinate/Romance-origin words** (inherently melodic vowel patterns, obscure to registrars)
- **Musical/poetic terminology** (niche enough to avoid premium flags)
- **Rare English words with liquid consonants** (l, r, m, n — the most melodic consonant class)
- **Foreign loanwords** English speakers find beautiful but wouldn't think to register

## Phonetic Patterns That Sound Beautiful

1. **Liquid onset** (L/R start): lumen, lyra, revel, rille — 24 words in list

4. **Nasal resonance** (m, n interior): lumen, nimbi, ambit — creates hum
5. **Diphthong richness** (two-vowel glides): moire, poise, gleam — complex vowel
