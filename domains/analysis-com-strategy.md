## 1. Current State of the .COM Market

### The Numbers

- **375 million** domains registered globally (Q3 2025)
- **160+ million** of those are .com
- **.com market size:** $2.4B+ (2024), projected $3B+ by 2033
- **2025 domain sales:** 190,300 transactions totaling $314.7M — a **67% increase** from 2024
- **.com dollar volume:** Up ~32% vs 2024

### What's Left to Register at Base Price

| Length | .COM Availability | Reality |
|--------|------------------|---------|
| 2-letter | 0% available | All taken since the 1990s. Aftermarket: $50K-$1M+ |
| 3-letter | 0% available | All 17,576 combinations registered decades ago. Aftermarket: $50K-$500K+ |
| 4-letter (all alpha) | 0% available | All 456,976 combinations registered. Aftermarket: $10K-$100K |
| 4-letter (with digits) | ~770,795 unregistered | Digit-containing combos like "x3k9.com" — mostly worthless |
| 5-letter (dictionary words) | 0% available | All 8,938 English 5-letter words are taken |
| 5-letter (nonsense/invented) | **YES — thousands available** | 11.88M possible combos, many pronounceable ones still open |
| 6-letter | **YES — many available** | Sweet spot for invented brandable names |
| 7+ letter | **YES — wide open** | Two-word compounds, invented words readily available |

### The Brutal Truth

Every meaningful English word in .com is taken. Every short acronym is taken. Every common name, place, verb, noun, and adjective is taken. What remains falls into three categories:

1. **Invented/nonsense words** (5-7 letters, pronounceable) — the goldmine
2. **Compound words** (two short words jammed together) — still viable
3. **Expired domains** dropping back into the pool — requires tools and speed

## 4. The .COM vs Alternative TLD Decision

### .COM — Still King

- **53% of YC W2024 startups** chose .com
- **85% of successful startups** use .com
- Customers type ".com" by default — brand leakage is real if you don't own it
- Best email deliverability
- Highest resale value
- No geopolitical risk

### .AI — Hot But Expensive and Risky

- **908,000+ registrations** (late 2025), on track for 1M+ in 2026
- **$70-82/year** — 7-8x .com cost
- 3-5x price appreciation on premium .ai names over 3 years
- **30% of recent YC cohorts** now use .ai
- Controlled by Anguilla (population ~15,000) — revenue bonanza for them
- Notable sales: cloud.ai ($600K), wisdom.ai ($750K), blockchain.ai ($405K)
- Risk: If AI hype cycle cools, .ai premium could deflate

### .IO — Danger Zone

- **Geopolitical risk is REAL:** UK signed treaty (May 2025) transferring Chagos Islands to Mauritius
- British Indian Ocean Territory (.io) may cease to exist as an ISO country code
- IANA protocol: 5-year retirement window after ISO removal, extendable to 10 years max
- Mauritius might maintain .io for revenue, but no guarantees
- **Verdict:** Do not invest in .io for long-term holds. Use it if you must, but own the .com backup.

### .CO, .DEV, .APP — Niche Players

- **.co:** Decent brandability, affordable ($25/yr), some confusion with .com typos (could be good or bad)
- **.dev/.app:** Google-backed, stable, cheap ($15/yr), but limited to tech audience
- None of these carry meaningful resale value compared to .com

### Verdict

**Buy .com when possible.** Period. For AI projects specifically, .ai is acceptable as a primary with .com redirect ownership. Avoid .io for new investments.

Since every real English word .com is taken, invent a new word. Make it pronounceable, memorable, 5-7 characters. Register it at base price ($10). Build a brand on it.

## 6. Expired Domain Strategy

### How It Works

1. Domain owner doesn't renew
2. Domain enters grace period (30-45 days)
3. Domain enters redemption period (30 days, ~$80 fee to reclaim)
4. Domain enters pending delete (5 days)
5. Domain drops — available for registration or caught by backorder services

### The Tools

| Platform | Backorder Cost | Auction Min | Best For | Catch Rate |
|----------|---------------|-------------|----------|------------|
| DropCatch | $59-69 (paid on success) | $69 | Drop-catching | Highest (1,200+ registrar network) |
| NameJet | $69-79 | $69 | Premium expired names | Good (shares inventory with SnapNames) |
| SnapNames | $69-79 | $69 | Same as NameJet | Same pool as NameJet — never backorder both |
| GoDaddy | $4.99 membership | Varies | Largest inventory | Good for common names |
| Dynadot | $5 min spend | $5-30 (declining) | Budget entry | Lower, but cheapest option |
| Sedo | Free membership | $79 | International | Moderate |
| ExpiredDomains.net | Free (monitoring only) | N/A | Research/filtering | Tool, not a catch service |

### What Expired Domains Cost

- **Average expired domain at auction:** $50-$500
- **Domains with good backlinks/history:** $500-$5,000
- **Premium expired .com (short, dictionary):** $5,000-$50,000+
- **DropCatch backorder fee:** $59-69 (only if caught)

### Strategy for Expired Domains

1. **Monitor with ExpiredDomains.net** — Filter by length (5-7 chars), TLD (.com), backlinks (DA 10+)
2. **Place backorders on DropCatch** for domains with SEO value (existing backlinks, domain age)
3. **Watch Dynadot closeouts** for cheap catches ($5-$30 declining price auctions)
4. **Never backorder the same domain on both NameJet AND SnapNames** — identical inventory, you'll bid against yourself
5. **Budget $60-100 per attempt**, expect to lose most auctions on good names

### Honest Assessment

Expired domain catching is competitive and dominated by professionals with automated tools and bulk capital. As an individual, you can pick up decent names, but the premium drops attract dozens of backorders. Think of it as a supplementary strategy, not your primary play.

## 10. Final Verdict — Brutally Honest

### For Brand Builders (You're Building Something)

The invented-word .com strategy is **excellent**. You can register a great brandable .com for $10 today. The value isn't in the domain — it's in what you build on it. Spotify, Figma, Canva — all invented words that became billion-dollar brands. The domain cost was irrelevant. **Do this.**

### For Domain Investors (You're Flipping)

The math is brutal:
- 97% of domains never sell
- 1-3% annual sell-through
- Median sale: $1,500-$3,000
- You're competing against professionals with 10,000+ domain portfolios and automated tools
- Renewal fees will eat you alive if you're not disciplined

**Success requires:** Deep keyword research, data-driven selection, ruthless pruning, 2-5 year holding patience, and accepting that most of your picks will be worth nothing. This is not passive income — it's a skilled trade with lottery-like variance.

### The Bottom Line

If you need a domain for a project, the invented-word .com strategy gives you a great name for $10. If you're looking to invest in domains for profit, treat it like any other business: you need edge, capital, patience, and the willingness to be wrong 97% of the time.
