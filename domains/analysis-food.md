## Strategy

Premium domain pricing correlates with **consumer recognition** and **commercial intent**. Words like "brew", "cook", "bake", "mint" are premium because they're everyday vocabulary with obvious brand appeal. Our confirmed non-premium words (hops, pine, plum, fork, foam, flax, soot, petal) share a pattern: they're **real English words** but slightly **niche or archaic** -- known to enthusiasts but not to mass-market registrars' premium lists.

This list targets the same sweet spot: legitimate, evocative words that fly under premium radar.
