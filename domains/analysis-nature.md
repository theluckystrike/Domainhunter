## Selection Strategy

### The Premium Detection Pattern

From price-checking ~40 words across 12 TLDs, the pattern is clear:

**PREMIUM words** share these traits:
- Immediately evocative, aspirational, or "brand-ready" (silk, gold, glow, pure, bold, soft)
- Common adjectives that modify the TLD naturally (pure.skin, soft.hair, bold.beauty)
- Gemstone/precious material names (ruby, jade, gold)
- Pop-culture crossover words (epic, boss, ghost, wolf, star)

**NON-PREMIUM words** share these traits:
- Real English but require domain knowledge to know (flax, wren, tilth, loam)
- Nouns rather than adjectives (foam, hops, pine, dew, fork)
- Specific/technical rather than generic/aspirational
- Not commonly used in branding or marketing copy
- Often monosyllabic Old English or Latin botanical terms

### The Sweet Spot Formula

```
CHEAP = real word + uncommon + specific + not an adjective + not aspirational
PREMIUM = real word + common + generic + adjective/gemstone + aspirational
```
