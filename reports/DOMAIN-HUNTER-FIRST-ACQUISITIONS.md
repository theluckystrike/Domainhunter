## Selection Process

All 43 domains were scored across 9 dimensions:

- Domain length (shorter = more valuable)
- Hyphens (kill resale value)
- Suffix baggage ("inc", "corp", "ltd" = buyers don't want someone else's corporate name)
- Dictionary words (highest intrinsic value)
- Brandability (pronounceable? memorable?)
- Niche keywords (health, tech, bio = premium)
- Original company funding (press backlinks correlate with funding)
- Sector value (biotech/cleantech/fintech command higher prices)
- Flip potential (would someone pay $500+ for this?)

### What We Eliminated

- **12 hyphenated domains** (c8-inc.com, nbo-tv.com, wi-chi.com, etc.) — hyphens are a resale killer, -20 points each
- **9 with corporate suffixes** (viacorinc.com, tgrxinc.com, chronicityinc.com, etc.) — buyers don't want to inherit a dead company's "Inc" branding
- **14 over 15 characters** (officialvirtualdj.com at 20 chars, trinitybiosystems.com at 19, etc.) — too long for premium resale
- **3 with geographic limiters** (g10korea.com, alamakespana.com) — limits global buyer pool

That left ~5 viable candidates.

- **Zero competition.** RDAP 404 confirmed, Dynadot search confirmed available. Nobody else is looking at this.

## Pick #2: neovistainc.com — $10.99

### Why This Domain

Different thesis from viryd.com. This pick is about **inherited SEO equity from a $130M funded company.**

- **NeoVista Inc. raised $130M** in venture funding — the highest-funded company with an available domain in our entire pool.
- **Press backlinks are real.** $130M in biotech funding generates coverage on TechCrunch, FierceBiotech, BioPharma Dive, SEC filings, Crunchbase, PitchBook. Those backlinks still point to neovistainc.com. When we own the domain, we inherit them.
- **"NeoVista" is a clean brand name.** Latin roots (neo = new, vista = view). Sounds professional, medical, forward-looking.
- **Biotech buyer pool.** Pharmaceutical companies, biotech startups, and medical device companies regularly acquire brandable domain names. The biotech naming convention (Latin/Greek compounds) makes this a natural fit.

### The "inc" Suffix Trade-off

Yes, "inc" reduces generic resale value. A buyer named "NeoVista LLC" or "NeoVista Labs" might not want "neovistainc.com". But:

1. The $130M funding backstory means the name has real recognition in biotech circles
2. The SEO equity (backlinks from major publications) has tangible value
3. At $10.99, the downside is literally $10.99. The upside is $500-$5,000.

### Estimated Value

- Conservative: $100-$300 (quick flip, "inc" discount)
- Moderate: $500-$1,500 (biotech buyer, values the backlinks)
- Aggressive: $2,000-$5,000 (exact-match buyer who IS NeoVista Inc or similar)
