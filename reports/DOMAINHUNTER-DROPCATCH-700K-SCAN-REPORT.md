## KEY INSIGHTS

1. **No single-word .com premium domains** exist in the drop list. All valuable single-word .com domains get caught by professional drop-catchers or renewed. The "word .com" matches found (akund, almuce, papess) are obscure dictionary words with no commercial value.

2. **AI + [Industry] domains are the richest vein.** LegalGpt.io, AiMortgageGuru.com, AiFunnel.io, AiMarketingTools.io — the pattern of combining "AI" with a specific vertical is consistently producing the highest-value names.

3. **.io remains the premium startup TLD.** 3,853 .io domains dropping, including 10 three-letter names. Real English word .io domains (Represent.io, Phrasing.io, Diffused.io) are the hidden gems.

4. **Finance keywords command the highest CPC.** Insurance ($15-50), mortgage ($10-45), loans ($8-40) — even mediocre finance domains outperform premium domains in other niches for ad revenue.

5. **Compound .com domains are the real opportunity.** No premium single-word .com exists in the drop, but 173 clean two-word compounds like hotwave.com, datatale.com, megastack.com have real brand potential at $100-$2,000.

6. **Zero .ai, .co, or .dev domains** in the entire dataset. These TLDs have separate drop channels.

7. **The 700K list refreshes daily.** New domains enter pendingDelete continuously. Running the scanner daily on fresh CSVs will surface different opportunities. Our monitored targets will eventually appear here.
