### Domain-by-Domain Analysis

#### globalgeopark.org — TIER 1 CONFIRMED (bid raised $350 → $400)

**What verified:**
- DataForSEO confirms 814 keywords globally, $3,623 ETV
- US: 249 keywords, 46 in top-10, $626 ETV
- Top keywords: "geological park" (pos 5, SV 4,400), "geoparks" (pos 8, SV 4,400)
- Clean educational niche, no trademark issues

**What was debunked:**
- UNESCO.org does NOT link to this domain. UNESCO links to globalgeoparksnetwork.org instead
- Domain is China-based (Beijing address), not the official Global Geoparks Network

**Verdict:** UNESCO backlink was false, but 814 real keywords with verified ETV is the actual value. Only domain worth aggressive pursuit.

#### devhub.io — DEBUNKED (bid slashed $500 → $75)

**DOUBLE DEBUNK:**
1. **ethereum.org backlink DOES NOT EXIST.** Verified by directly checking ethereum.org/developers/tools/. devhub.io links TO ethereum repos, but ethereum.org does NOT link BACK. The link direction was misunderstood in Sprint 6.
2. **ZERO keywords, ZERO traffic** in DataForSEO despite DR 27 and 275 RD.

**Action:** CANCEL the $500 offer immediately. The entire offer was based on a false premise.

#### jerusalemonline.com — DEBUNKED (bid slashed $250 → $50)

**Finding:** DataForSEO shows ZERO keywords, ZERO organic traffic. The 4,564 referring domains and 2.78M backlinks reported by HypeStat produce no rankings whatsoever.

**Red flag:** 99.4% follow ratio (2.76M follow vs 17K nofollow) is abnormally high — classic spam indicator. A 28-year-old domain with millions of backlinks and zero rankings = either Google penalty or stale/fabricated metrics.

#### fiskerinc.com — REMOVED (bid $200 → $0)

**Triple disqualification:**
1. **TRADEMARK:** "Fisker" is a registered personal name trademark. Bankruptcy estate controls IP.
2. **DOMAIN LOCK:** clientRenewProhibited means domain likely won't drop — controlled by estate.
3. **WASTING ASSET:** All 10 keywords are navigational for a dead brand, declining 50%/quarter.

#### sushifaq.com — Maintained T2, bid halved ($150 → $75)

**Finding:** Only $6.22 ETV, 20 keywords globally, best position is #40. Zero top-30 keywords. DA 51 cannot be reconciled with near-zero performance. However: 26-year age, clean food niche, 15 new keywords appearing. Worth a speculative low bid.

#### lagerhousedetroit.com — Downgraded T4 ($150 → $30)

**Finding:** This is a MUSIC VENUE, not a restaurant. DA 53 with only 89 RD from directories (TripAdvisor, Yelp, DoorDash). Zero food content backlinks. Zero organic traffic. Local directory links provide no redirect value.
