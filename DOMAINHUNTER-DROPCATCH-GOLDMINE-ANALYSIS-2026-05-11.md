## CRITICAL INSIGHT: WHY MOST OF THESE ARE TRASH

From analyzing 200+ domains in the listing:

1. **Private Seller listings at $59 are speculator inventory** -- domainers bought these for $10 and list at $59 hoping to flip. 99.91% get zero bids because they have zero keyword backing.

2. **The domains worth having** sit in the Pending Delete feed (817K domains) and require DA/backlink filtering to find. This is where REVENANT's automated pipeline shines.

3. **The 793 domains with bids (0.09%)** are either LLLL.com commodity plays or rare keyword-match finds. The rest is noise.

4. **EMDs still work in 2026** -- domains need ~60% lower DR to rank in top 10 vs non-EMDs. But ONLY if the domain matches a real search query that real humans type into Google.

5. **YMYL (Your Money Your Life) is the wall** -- legal, health, finance domains CAN'T rank without genuine E-E-A-T. The exceptions are domains targeting informational/tool intent (calculators, guides) rather than transactional intent (buy insurance, hire attorney).
